## Course correction (2026-05-07)

Manual GUI testing revealed the slice's stated goal — "the cloth simulator actually responds to forces" — was not actually delivered by the original commit (`4047af0`). The cloth force kernels (`compute_cloth_grid_forces_fast`, `compute_tri_spring_forces` in `src/metal/physics.metal`) hardcoded gravity from `SimParams::G` and ignored the `externalForces` buffer the C++ side was binding at slot 7. PLAN.md's "no `.metal` file changes" non-goal was based on the incorrect assumption that the kernels already read `externalForces`; they did not (CM-004). Both kernels updated to read `externalForces[id]` instead of a hardcoded gravity vector. Air resistance (`vel * kair`) stays as an internal drag.

This crosses BDD-103's renderer/GUI/scene-IO boundary line *only* in the sense that the simulation-pipeline change was needed to make the GUI panel actually reach the simulator — but the change is not "introducing a new behavior or pipeline", it is plumbing an already-bound buffer into the kernel that was supposed to consume it. Estimator should treat this as a corrective kernel patch, not a backend-boundary violation.
