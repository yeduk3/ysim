- **Build-time discovery (no slice expansion).** Two issues surfaced during Block 25 authoring; both fixed locally without altering scope:
  1. **`Framebuffer::attachTexture2D(int, GLint, ...)` only auto-derives format+type for `GL_RGBA32F`** (see `framebuffer.hpp::TextureFormat::generate`). My first call `fbo.attachTexture2D(1, GL_RGBA8, 256, 256)` produced `GL_INVALID_ENUM` and "Framebuffer is not complete!". Fixed by constructing the TextureFormat explicitly: `{internalFormat=GL_RGBA8, format=GL_RGBA, type=GL_UNSIGNED_BYTE}`. Documented inline in the Block 25 comment. NOT a `Framebuffer` modification — the harness uses the existing explicit-TextureFormat overload.
  2. **Non-metallic material at low roughness produces max-diff ≤ 5 across the image** (originally tested with baseColor=red, metallic=0). At metallic=0 F0=0.04 (4% specular at normal incidence), and GGX's peak at roughness=0.1 has `D = 1/(π·a²) ≈ 3000` at exactly NdotH=1 but collapses to <1 at NdotH=0.95 — the hotspot is sub-pixel-thin in rasterized output. Most fragments see only diffuse + ~0 specular; the two renders are nearly identical. Switched to metallic=1 silver-ish (`baseColor=(0.85, 0.85, 0.85)`) so F0=baseColor amplifies specular by 20×; max-diff lands at ~100+ byte values, easily clearing the threshold of 30. Documented in D-032 + the harness comment.
